Player Wiki: Base Operations Guide

1. Core Mechanics

Builders: Construction requires builders. Your total builder capacity is determined by your Headquarters (HQ) and any Worker Huts you build. If a building is currently under construction, it occupies a builder.

Resources: Your base relies on two main resources:

  • Steel: Used for basic construction. Generated passively by Steel Mines.
  • Oil: Used for advanced structures and unit management. Generated passively by Oil Pumps.

2. Structures

  • Headquarters (HQ): The heart of your base. Generates initial builders and determines your maximum expansion potential.
  • Worker Hut: Provides additional builders, allowing you to construct multiple buildings simultaneously.
  • Steel Mine & Oil Pump: Passive resource generators. Upgrade them to increase output rate.
  • Supply Depot: Increases your total resource storage capacity.

3. Military & Defense

  • Barracks: Allows you to train Soldiers. Vital for overworld map expansion and defense.
  • Medic Station: Allows you to train Medics, who support your Soldiers during combat missions.
  • Gunship Pad: Required to deploy troops across the World Map rapidly.
  • Defense Turret: Automatically targets and damages incoming threats to your base. Placing them strategically protects vital resource nodes.

4. Building & Deconstructing

Use the Build Structures menu in the bottom left to select a building. Valid placements will show in green, invalid in red. After placing, click the green checkmark (✓) to confirm. If you make a mistake, you can use the Deconstruct option in the build menu to remove a structure and receive a 75% refund of its cost.
