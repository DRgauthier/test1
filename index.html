Player Tips & Wiki

Core Mechanics

Resources: Steel and Oil are the primary resources used to build and upgrade your base.

Builders: You start with 1 Builder from your Headquarters. Constructing buildings and upgrading them occupies 1 Builder until the timer completes. Expand your Builder capacity by building Worker Huts.

Upgrades: Click on an existing building to open its Upgrade menu. Buildings stop functioning (generating resources, housing units) while they are under construction. The maximum level of any building is determined by the level of your Headquarters.

Structures

  • Headquarters: The heart of your base. Provides 1 Builder. Upgrading it increases the level cap for all other buildings.
  • Worker Hut: Each completed Worker Hut permanently adds +1 Builder to your global capacity, allowing you to build and upgrade more structures simultaneously.
  • Steel Mine: Passively generates Steel over time. Upgrading it increases the generation rate (1.5x per level).
  • Oil Pump: Passively generates Oil over time. Upgrading it increases the generation rate (1.5x per level).
  • Supply Depot: Increases the maximum storage capacity for both Steel and Oil by 500 per depot.
  • Barracks: Allows you to train Soldier units for combat. Provides a base capacity of 10 Soldiers.
  • Medic Station: Allows you to train Medic units to heal your troops. Provides a base capacity of 5 Medics.
  • Gunship Pad: Unlocks the ability to deploy troops to the World Map via Gunships for territory control missions.
  • Defense Turret: A stationary automated turret that defends your base against incoming threats.
